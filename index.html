Past Simple in IT

Read the notes, then practise. Type your answers in the exercises and use the buttons under each one to check your work.

A

Study this example

Maya Chen is a software engineer. Last year, she joined a product team at a fintech company. She learned a new programming language, fixed several critical bugs and wrote automated tests. She was responsible for the payment feature, and she helped the team release it on time.

joined / learned / fixed / wrote / was / helped are all Past Simple forms.

 

B

Regular and irregular verbs

Very often, the Past Simple ends in -ed (regular verbs):

  • I work on the mobile app now. Before that, I worked on the company website.
  • The team tested the new feature, but they postponed the release.
  • Our developer fixed the login bug yesterday.
  • Nina documented the API because the team needed clear instructions.

For spelling rules (for example, fixed, tested, deployed), follow the usual -ed spelling patterns.

But many verbs are irregular. The Past Simple does not end in -ed. For example:

  • write→wroteOur team wrote automated tests for the new feature.
  • see→sawI saw an error message after the deployment.
  • go→wentWe went through the bug reports this morning.
  • build→builtThey built a prototype in two days.

For a list of irregular verbs, see an irregular-verbs reference list.

 

C

Questions and negative sentences

In questions and negative sentences, use did / didn't + the infinitive (base form):

PositiveQuestionNegative
I fixed the bug.Did you fix the bug?I didn't fix the bug.
She saw the error.Did she see the error?She didn't see the error.
They deployed the update.Did they deploy the update?They didn't deploy the update.
  • The team tested the update. Did the team test the update?
  • How many bugs did they find during testing?
  • I didn't change the code because I didn't understand the requirement.
  • "Did you deploy the fix?" "No, I didn't."

Sometimes do is the main verb (did you do?, I didn't do):

  • What did you do after the stand-up meeting? (not: What did you after the stand-up meeting?)
  • I didn't do anything because the build was still running. (not: I didn't anything)

 

D

The past of be (am / is / are)

SubjectPositive / NegativeQuestion

  • Were the requirements clear when you started the task?
